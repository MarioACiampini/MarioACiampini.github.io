My research sits at the interface of **optomechanics**, **quantum information** and **stochastic and quantum thermodynamics**. I am an experimentalist: I build optical-tweezer platforms that levitate nanoparticles in vacuum, and I develop the tools to control them, from programmable optical potentials to fast feedback electronics.

<div class="topic" id="quantum-control" markdown="1">
<span class="topic-tag">01 · Current</span>
## Quantum control of levitated nanoparticles

Can a glass sphere about a hundred nanometres across be put into a spatial quantum superposition? I develop fast, programmable control of the optical potential that holds a nanoparticle, so that its wavefunction can expand in a nonlinear potential and show matter-wave interference on short timescales ([PNAS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2306953121)). In parallel, I am building an experiment that uses FPGA-based control to generate genuine quantum squeezing of the centre-of-mass motion of a levitated particle.
</div>

<div class="topic" id="thermodynamics" markdown="1">
<span class="topic-tag">02 · Current</span>
## Thermodynamics of information

Landauer's principle says that erasing one bit of information costs at least *k*<sub>B</sub>*T* ln 2 of work. A levitated particle in a double-well potential is an ideal one-bit memory to test this. With spatio-temporal control of the optical potential, I showed that a memory starting **out of equilibrium** can be erased at a cost below the Landauer bound, using the non-equilibrium state as a thermodynamic resource ([Phys. Rev. Research 2025](https://doi.org/10.1103/r81x-zblx)). This line of work, started as my FWF Lise Meitner project, now looks at optimal memories and entropy production in nonlinear potentials.
</div>

<div class="topic" id="interacting" markdown="1">
<span class="topic-tag">03 · Current</span>
## Interacting levitated systems & feedback

Going beyond a single particle, we demonstrated tunable light-induced dipole–dipole coupling between two levitated nanoparticles ([Science 2022](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abp9941)). I also work on the enabling technology: clean delivery of nanoparticles into ultra-high vacuum through hollow-core fibres ([APL 2024](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0190658)), and, with the Automation and Control Institute of TU Wien, real-time feedback that stabilises a particle at the intensity *minimum* of an optical potential ([Opt. Express 2024](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.541267), [Control Eng. Practice 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conengprac.2025.106665)).
</div>

<div class="topic" id="photonics" markdown="1">
<span class="topic-tag">04 · Earlier work</span>
## Photonic quantum information

During my PhD and postdoc in Rome, I generated, certified and used **hyperentangled** and **cluster states** of photons, both in bulk optics and on femtosecond-laser-written chips ([Light Sci. Appl. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/lsa.2016.64)). I used them for multiparameter quantum metrology ([PRL 2017](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.130504)), to measure the capacities of quantum channels, to extract work from multipartite entanglement, and to observe signatures of quantum Darwinism.
</div>
